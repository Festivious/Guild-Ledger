# Guild Ledger: Bounties and Analytics - Changelog

## 0.3.0-alpha (October 1, 2026)

**Heads up: the whole guild needs to update together.** This version can't talk to the old one. Your saved data carries over.

### Looking back at your play

- The map groups your play the way you actually played: the camps where you fought a lot, and the trips in between.
- Every fight has a new **Mobs** tab. Each enemy gets its own card: who started it, how hard it hit, and whether it was an add that joined late.
- Fixed kills going missing or showing up in the wrong fight. Sessions you already recorded are fixed too.
- A new session starts when you change zones, but never mid-flight or mid-fight.
- The window opens straight into your latest session, and side panels no longer cover the map.

### Your privacy

- You choose which sessions to keep. Archived ones are locked with a key that never leaves your computer.
- Older sessions are archived for you and kept for 30 days, unless you mark one to keep.
- Nothing is sent unless you claim a bounty that asks for it. If you start a claim and never send it, the leftover copy is cleaned up.

### Bounties

- Every bounty shows now (the limit of 12 is gone), each with its icon.
- Writing a bounty is easier. Post unlocks as soon as there's a subject, and the last page is tidier, with simple names for what you're asking players to share. Hover a name to see what it covers.
- You can pick other officers to help read and approve claims, and the bounty lists who gets what.
- On your own bounty, Claims, Edit and Remove sit together at the bottom.
- Claims aren't lost if an officer logs off or crashes, and they only count once the mail has actually gone out.
- Guild quest claims carry their own proof, so officers don't have to count by hand.
- Key letters open by themselves at the mailbox.

### Officers

- Roles only count while the person is in the guild, and each guild keeps its own list.
- Officer details spread more reliably, and bounty numbers never repeat.
